| SYS-001 Cleaning area per charge | BASE-005 Battery capacity | The mission energy analysis shows that 80 m² at an average of 2.6 A takes 4875 mAh. With a 15 % reserve for the return to the dock this needs a 6400 mAh pack. |
| SYS-011 Overall height | BASE-004 Base module height; TOP-001 Top module height | The overall height is the base module up to the parting line plus the top module above it. The split at 57 mm follows the drive wheel diameter and the battery pack; the remaining 38 mm is for the shell and the scanner turret. |
| SYS-015 Robot mass | BASE-003 Base module mass; BIN-001 Dust bin mass; HAR-001 Wiring harness mass; PCB-002 Main board mass; TOP-002 Top module mass | Every gram of the robot belongs to one of five budgets, and the budgets add up to the 4.0 kg limit. Margin is held inside each module budget, so a module that grows has to renegotiate with the others rather than use up a common reserve unnoticed. |
| SYS-016 Recharge time | BASE-006 Charge current | Recharging 85 % of 6400 mAh within 4 hours, including the constant-voltage tail, needs at least 2 A in the constant-current phase. |
| SYS-SAFE-011 Safe stop on hazard | BASE-001 Cliff sensing latency; BASE-002 Drive braking time; FW-001 Safety supervisor deadline; PCB-001 Drive disable latency | The 100 ms from hazard to standstill is a chain of four steps in three modules: sensing 25 ms, supervisor decision 10 ms, drive disable 5 ms and braking 50 ms. The budgets add up to 90 ms, which leaves 10 ms unallocated margin. |
