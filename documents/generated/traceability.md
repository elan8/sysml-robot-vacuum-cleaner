| Customer requirement | Derived system requirements | Why this set is complete |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USR-001 Whole-home cleaning in one run | SYS-001 Cleaning area per charge; SYS-002 Floor coverage | Cleaning the whole home in one run needs enough energy for the area and a path that actually reaches the floor. Area per charge bounds the first, coverage of the reachable floor the second. |
| USR-002 Unattended docking and charging | SYS-007 Return to dock on low battery; SYS-008 Charging within the battery safe operating area; SYS-016 Recharge time | Being ready the next day without help needs the robot to leave for the dock early enough, to charge safely once there, and to finish charging overnight. |
| USR-003 Reliable navigation at home | SYS-004 Localization accuracy; SYS-005 Coverage map maintenance | Knowing where it is becomes a bound on localization error. Knowing where it has cleaned becomes a maintained coverage map. |
| USR-004 Mission progress and fault feedback | SYS-009 Mission status reporting | Progress and faults are both carried by the mission status the robot reports to the application. |
| USR-005 Privacy of the home map | SYS-010 Local storage of map data | The map is the only personal data the robot creates. Keeping it local by default, with transfer only after explicit consent, is the whole need. |
| USR-006 Cleaning under furniture | SYS-011 Overall height | Fitting under furniture with about 10 cm of clearance is one dimension, the overall height including the LiDAR turret, with margin for carpet pile. |
| USR-007 Quiet operation | SYS-012 Acoustic noise | A conversation in the same room is possible below about 65 dB(A), so the need becomes one sound pressure limit with a stated distance, floor and mode. |
| USR-008 Easy routine maintenance | SYS-013 Dust bin capacity | Emptying at most once per run needs a bin that holds the debris of one run of the reference home; doing it without tools is part of the same requirement. |
| USR-009 Thresholds between rooms | SYS-014 Threshold climbing | Door thresholds and rug edges in the target homes are up to 20 mm high; crossing them in both directions is one capability. |
| USR-010 Carrying between floors | SYS-015 Robot mass | Carrying in one hand is bounded by mass. The grip is a detail of the enclosure design under that limit. |
| USR-SAFE-001 Safe operation in the household | SYS-003 Obstacle avoidance; SYS-006 Wheel stall detection and recovery; SYS-SAFE-010 Cliff detection; SYS-SAFE-011 Safe stop on hazard; SYS-SAFE-012 Battery protection independent of firmware | The household hazards are falling, collision, entanglement and the battery. Each has a detection requirement, and all of them lead to one supervised safe stop. The battery additionally has protection that does not depend on firmware, because a firmware fault must not become a fire risk. |
