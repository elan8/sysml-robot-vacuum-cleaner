This document is generated from the SysML v2 model of the robot. Requirement tables and diagrams are projected from the model at the revision stated on the title page, so they agree with it by construction; the surrounding text is written by the engineering team.

Requirements are identified by a stable number. Numbers starting with **USR** are customer requirements and numbers starting with **SYS** are system requirements. The word *shall* marks a binding requirement.

The companion documents are the Customer Requirements Specification (RVC-CRS-001), the System Requirements Specification (RVC-SRS-001) and the System Design Specification (RVC-SDS-001).
