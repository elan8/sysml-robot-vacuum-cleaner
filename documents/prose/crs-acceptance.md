The customer requirements are accepted when the household scenarios of this document have been demonstrated with a production-representative robot in a reference home. Verifying the system requirements of RVC-SRS-001 is necessary but not sufficient: validation against the customer requirements is assessed separately.

No validation has been carried out for this revision.

Open points:

- The size and layout of the reference home used for validation are to be agreed.
- The needs of the service technician are covered only through fault reporting; dedicated service requirements may be added.
- The noise the household accepts is stated as holding a conversation in the same room; the measurement condition is fixed in RVC-SRS-001 and is to be confirmed with a user panel.
