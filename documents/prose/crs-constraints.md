The customer requirements assume the following conditions of use. A requirement is not claimed outside them.

- The robot is used indoors, in a home, on hard floors and low-pile carpet.
- The docking station is connected to mains power and placed against a wall with free floor in front of it.
- Doors between the rooms to be cleaned are open, and the floor is free of liquids.
- The mobile application is installed on a device on the same home network as the robot.

The following constraints apply to any solution:

- The robot must be safe to operate around people and pets without supervision.
- Maps of the home are personal data. They stay on the robot and in the home unless the user explicitly agrees to share them.
- The product is designed around components that are commercially available from more than one distributor.
