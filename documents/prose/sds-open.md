This design is a teaching and tool-validation baseline. It is not a certified product design, and its bill of materials is not released for production.

- The analysis inputs are estimates. They must be replaced by measured values before the analyses are used as verification evidence.
- The safe-stop design values add up to 72 ms against a 100 ms limit; the braking time dominates and should be confirmed on hardware first.
- Costs in the bill of materials are budgetary estimates, and several purchased parts do not yet have a selected manufacturer part.
- The mass margin is 9 percent of the limit, which is small for this stage of a design.
- The firmware architecture defines tasks, queues and timing, not their internal design.
- Component lifecycle status is recorded as checked on a date and is not monitored.
