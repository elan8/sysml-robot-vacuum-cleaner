The requirements refer to the following limit values. They are defined once in the model ({{DesignLimits::cleaningAreaTarget}} and its siblings) and used by the requirements, the analyses and the verification cases, so a change to a limit propagates to all three.

- Cleaning area on one charge: at least 80 m².
- Coverage of the reachable floor: at least 90 percent.
- Smallest obstacle to be avoided: 30 mm high.
- Smallest drop to be detected: 50 mm.
- Reaction time from hazard to safe stop: at most 100 ms.
- Localization error during the reference mission: at most 150 mm.
- Time to detect a stalled wheel: at most 2 s.
- State of charge at which the robot returns to the dock: 15 percent.
- Battery capacity budget: 12 500 mAh.
