The plan covers the robot: its mechanics, electronics and firmware, at three levels.

- **Module verification** against the module requirements of RVC-SDS-001.
- **System verification** against the system requirements of RVC-SRS-001.
- **Validation** against the customer requirements of RVC-CRS-001.

The docking station and the mobile application take part as test equipment. Their own verification, production acceptance testing of each manufactured unit, regulatory approval and reliability growth testing are outside this plan.
