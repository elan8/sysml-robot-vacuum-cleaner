The product is a battery-powered robot that vacuums the floors of a home without supervision and returns to its docking station to recharge. This specification covers the robot, its docking station as seen by the robot, and the mobile application through which the user starts a mission and follows its progress.

The following are outside the scope:

- cloud services and remote access from outside the home;
- wet cleaning, stair climbing and outdoor use;
- product variants, accessories and consumables;
- manufacturing, packaging and regulatory approval.
