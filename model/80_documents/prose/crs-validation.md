A customer requirement is met when a household gets what it describes, in a home, from a production-representative robot. That is validation, and it is planned in RVC-VVP-001, which names a validation case for every requirement of this specification. Verifying the system requirements of RVC-SRS-001 is necessary but not sufficient.

No validation has been carried out for this revision.

Open points:

- The size and layout of the reference homes used for validation are to be agreed.
- The needs of the service technician are covered only through fault reporting; dedicated service requirements may be added.
- The noise the household accepts is stated as holding a conversation in the same room; the measurable limit is in RVC-SRS-001 and is to be confirmed with a user panel.
