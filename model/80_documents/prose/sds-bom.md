The bill of materials is generated from the physical architecture. Every part that is bought or made carries a classification in the model with its description, whether it is bought or made, and a unit cost; its mass is the attribute the mass budget uses. Quantities follow from the part usages, so four cliff sensors in the model are four on the list. Where the design has selected a catalogue part, the manufacturer and part number come from that part. The build fails for a physical part without a classification or a mass.

Costs are budgetary estimates at a volume of 10 000 units, in euro, for comparison between design options. They are not quotations. Fasteners, labels, packaging and the docking station are not in the model and not on the list. The same lines are written to `generated/bom.csv` for use in a spreadsheet.
