Six purchased components determine the design. Each is a catalogue part in the model with its manufacturer, part number and datasheet reference; the robot's own part definition selects it through a named dependency.

- **Microcontroller:** STMicroelectronics STM32U575VGT6 ({{PurchasedParts::STM32U575VGT6}}).
- **Motor driver:** Texas Instruments DRV8316R, one per drive motor ({{PurchasedParts::DRV8316R}}).
- **Time-of-flight sensor:** STMicroelectronics VL53L1CX, three fitted ({{PurchasedParts::VL53L1CX}}).
- **LiDAR:** Slamtec RPLIDAR C1 ({{PurchasedParts::RPLIDARC1}}).
- **Wireless module:** Espressif ESP32-C3-MINI-1 ({{PurchasedParts::ESP32C3MINI1}}).
- **Battery management:** Texas Instruments BQ40Z50-R2 ({{PurchasedParts::BQ40Z50R2}}).

Lifecycle status and availability were checked against the manufacturers' published data when the parts were entered and must be checked again before procurement.
